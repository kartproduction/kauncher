Privacy

What Kauncher does and does not do with your data. Back to download

The short version

  • No internet permission. Kauncher cannot send anything off your phone.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. We collect no data and share nothing.
  • Everything stays on your phone. Your notes, sketches, recordings, focus log, scores and settings are saved on your phone only.
  • Backups are yours. A backup is a code or file that you create and keep.

Permissions, and why

  • Camera: Kamera, the built-in camera, and quick capture. Photos stay on your phone.
  • Microphone: Kord, the built-in voice recorder. Recordings stay on your phone.
  • Usage access: The weekly report, phone time cap and app limits. It is worked out on your phone.
  • Notifications: Timers, reminders and the weekly report.
  • Do Not Disturb access: The optional setting that silences your phone during focus.
  • Set alarm: Starting timers with your phone's clock.
  • Wallpaper: The optional streak on your lock screen.
  • Uninstall apps: Removing an app from the All apps menu, when you choose to.
  • Run at start-up: Bringing back your widget and reminders after your phone restarts.

Each one is optional in the sense that the feature using it works only when you allow it. You can remove any permission in your phone's settings.

Links that open your browser

A few rows, such as Check for updates, Send feedback, Kutout, Kalendar and Mojeek, open a web page in your browser. Those pages belong to their own sites, and Kauncher sends nothing to them.
