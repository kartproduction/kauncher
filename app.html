Welcome to Kauncher

A calm home screen for people who make things.

  1. Write one line about what you are learning or creating today.
  2. Tap the timer to start a focus session and let the rest wait.
  3. Swipe up or tap All apps. Swipe down to come back home.
  4. Keep Kauncher on your Home Screen: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  5. Games and app suggestions are off to keep things quiet. Turn them on in All apps, under Kauncher, then Extras.
