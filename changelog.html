Changelog

What is new in each version of Kauncher. Back to download

Version 4.16

  • Focus calendar. Open Kettings, then Focus log, then Calendar. Each day of the month is shaded by how long you focused, from a light shade for a short day to a strong one for two hours or more. Tap a day to see its sessions, with the Nepali date. Use the arrows to go back through earlier months.
  • Colour themes. In Kettings, under Look, tap Color theme to choose Kauncher, Paper, Forest, Ocean, Sunset or Mono. Each has a light and a dark version and follows your Theme setting. Mono uses only black, white and gray, with no colour at all, so icons, lines and buttons stay in one tone.
  • Edit lock. In Calm tools, choose PIN or a wait of 1, 5 or 15 minutes. After that, opening Calm tools, changing the focus lengths in Kettings, changing an app’s pause, quiet or daily limit, and Restore folders all ask first. Enter your PIN or wait for the timer, and they stay open for 10 minutes. If you forget the PIN, you can wait 10 minutes instead. The lock lives inside Kauncher, so it cannot stop someone from uninstalling the app.

Nothing changed in permissions. Kauncher still has no internet permission.

Version 4.15

  • Focus hours. In Calm tools, switch on Scheduled focus and choose the days and hours, for example Sunday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM. During those hours Kauncher hides the apps you set to “Pause before opening” and says “Focus hours” on the home screen.
  • Bedtime mode. Choose when it starts and ends. In between, Kauncher turns gray and hides games, social apps and your “Pause before opening” apps, so the evening stays quiet. Apps return in the morning. Apps that do not say what they are may stay visible.
  • Nepali birthdays and anniversaries. Save a name with a Nepali date (month and day). It comes back every year, and the next one within 30 days shows on the home screen under the next festival. Dates work for Nepali years 2082 to 2088.

Nothing changed in permissions. Kauncher still has no internet permission.

Version 4.14

  • Pomodoro mode. Turn it on in Calm tools. Kauncher runs 25 minutes of focus, then a 5 minute break with a gentle sound, again and again. Every fourth break is 15 minutes. Tap the focus line any time to stop.
  • Sleep timer. In Calm tools, tap Sleep sound to play your focus sound (Ocean waves if none is chosen). It fades out and stops by itself after 15, 30 or 60 minutes, so it never plays all night.
  • Evening reflection. Choose a time between 6 and 10 PM. Once a day Kauncher asks “What did you make today?”. Your line is saved in Kotepad and shown in your weekly report.
  • Open counter. The pause screen now tells you how many times you opened that app from Kauncher today.

Nothing changed in permissions. Kauncher still has no internet permission.

Version 4.13

  • Five new focus sounds. Ocean waves: soft noise that swells and fades about every 10 seconds. Wind: dark, airy noise with slow gusts. Fire crackle: a low rumble with random little pops. Night crickets: two soft chirping voices. Warm hum: steady low tones with a gentle rumble. Rain, the ticking clock and soft noise are still there. Choose a sound in Calm tools. They are all made by the phone itself, so nothing is downloaded.

Nothing changed in permissions. Kauncher still has no internet permission.

Version 4.12

  • Kauncher in Nepali. When your phone is set to Nepali, the app now speaks Nepali almost everywhere: Kettings, the app drawer, the focus log, the weekly report, Calm tools, Kotepad, Kamera, Kord, backups, and the Kames list and game menus. A few messages that include numbers or names, such as scores and dates, are still in English.
  • The iPhone web app matches Android. New installs start calm, with games and app suggestions off, and see a short welcome guide. Under All apps, Kauncher, you can switch Extras on, reopen the guide, check for updates, send feedback and read the privacy page.
  • iPhone web app in Nepali. It follows your phone’s language too.
  • Fixed: stopping a focus session early. On the iPhone web app, a session you stopped early was logged as fully finished. It now logs only the time you actually focused.

Nothing changed in permissions. Kauncher still has no internet permission.

Version 4.11

  • A calmer start. On a new install, games, the Nepali date and app suggestions are off, so the home screen shows only what you are making today. Switch them on any time in Kettings under Extras. If you update from an older version, nothing changes for you.
  • Welcome guide. New installs see a short guide: how to write your line, start a focus timer, swipe up and down, and go back to your old launcher. You can open it again in Kettings under Help.
  • Check for updates and Send feedback. Two new rows in Kettings, under Help. They open our site and our issues page in your browser.
  • Privacy page. A plain list of what Kauncher does and does not do, and why it asks for each permission.
  • Nepali for the new screens. The welcome guide and the new Kettings rows appear in Nepali when your phone is set to Nepali.
  • Easier to use with a screen reader. Kettings rows and the welcome button are now announced as buttons.

Nothing changed in permissions. Kauncher still has no internet permission.

Version 4.10

  • Kutout, a free background remover. Kauncher now suggests Kutout, our app that removes the background from a photo in seconds. It is free, needs no sign-up, and your photos never leave your device.
  • Easy to find. Until Kutout is installed, a row in All apps takes you to its download page, the same way it does for Kalendar. It disappears once you install the app.
  • On iPhone too. The iPhone web app lists Kutout under Suggested and opens it in your browser.

Nothing changed in permissions. Kauncher still has no internet permission.

Version 4.9

  • Nepali date and next festival. Turn on “Show Nepali date and next festival” to see today’s date in the Nepali calendar (Bikram Sambat), with month names in Nepali, and the next Nepali festival or public holiday.
  • Festivals and holidays included. Dashain, Tihar, Holi, Maha Shivaratri, Buddha Jayanti, Janai Purnima, the Lhosar festivals, Nepali New Year and more, from Nepali year 2083 into 2084. Names show in English and in Nepali.
  • Works offline. The calendar is built into the app and needs no internet.

Nothing changed in permissions. Kauncher still has no internet permission.

Version 4.8

  • Keshey, a new game in Kames. Keshey is the traditional wrestling of Bhutan. The name means “holding the waist”: two wrestlers grab each other’s belts and try to throw the other down. Tap your side to push, but every tap costs balance, and balance only comes back when you pause. Throw your opponent out of the ring or make them lose their balance. First to win 2 rounds wins.
  • Play the computer, or two players on one phone. In two-player mode each player taps their own half of the screen, and both can tap at the same time. Kames remembers your wins and losses against the computer.
  • Six games in Kames. Tic-Tac-Toe, chess, Bagh-chal, Chungi, Keshey and Ludo. Search “Keshey” in All apps to open it.
  • On the web too. Keshey also works in the iPhone web app and in any browser, from the Kames page.

Nothing changed in permissions. Kauncher still has no internet permission.

Version 4.7

  • Chungi for up to four players. Choose 1, 2, 3 or 4 players before you start. With two or more players you take turns on one device: each turn is one chungi kept in the air until it drops, one point per kick. After three turns each, the most kicks in total wins.
  • Play Chungi against the computer. Turn on “Play against the computer” to add it as another player. It plays its own turns, and the longer a turn runs the more likely it is to miss.
  • Practice mode stays. With one player and no computer, Chungi works as before: keep it up as long as you can and Kames saves your best score.
  • On the web too. The same players and computer options work in the iPhone web app and in any browser.

Nothing changed in permissions. Kauncher still has no internet permission.

Version 4.6

  • Chungi, a new game in Kames. A traditional game from Nepal and northeastern India, played like hacky sack with a ball made of rubber bands tied in the middle. Tap to drop the chungi, then tap below the dashed line to kick it. Tap to the left of the ball to send it right, and the other way round. Each kick scores a point, and the game ends when the ball touches the ground. Kames keeps your best score. Chungi is for one player.
  • Five games in Kames. Tic-Tac-Toe, chess, Bagh-chal, Chungi and Ludo. Search “Chungi” in All apps to open it.
  • On the web too. Chungi also works in the iPhone web app and in any browser, from the Kames page.

Nothing changed in permissions. Kauncher still has no internet permission.

Version 4.5

  • Progress on the home screen. Today’s focus time and your streak show under the date. A small widget with the same two numbers can be added to any home screen that supports widgets.
  • Focus sounds. Rain, a ticking clock or soft noise, quietly during a session. They are made by the phone itself, so Kauncher still needs no internet permission. Set it in Calm tools.
  • Phone time cap. Set a daily cap such as 4 hours in the weekly report. Sunday’s report says whether you stayed under it, and a gentle nudge appears the day you pass it.
  • Intentions for the day. Choose up to three things to make today and tick them off. They show in the focus log and the weekly report, and they are included in backups.
  • Quick capture. Tap Krite to open a new note straight away. Hold Kord to start recording straight away.
  • Daily app limits. For example 30 minutes for one app. After the limit, the pause screen shows every time you open it that day. The weekly report shows on how many days you reached each limit and how often the pause appeared.
  • Pause screen reminds you. It shows your next unticked intention for the day.
  • Streak freeze. One missed day does not break your streak, at most once a week. You can turn it off.
  • What you made. Add a photo or file of work you made outside the phone, after a session or from the weekly report. The report shows a small gallery for the week.
  • Do not disturb during focus (optional). Your old setting comes back when the session ends.
  • Search shortcuts. Type n buy milk in All apps to save a note, or t 20 to start a 20 minute timer.
  • Streak on the lock screen (optional). Replaces your lock screen wallpaper with your streak.
  • Home screen choices. Hide the focus line or the intentions line in Calm tools.
  • Website. A changelog page, and the APK’s SHA-256 checksum so you can check your download.

Phone time cap and app limits need Usage access. Everything is worked out on your phone and nothing is uploaded.
